    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes, whenever a chair is free. Online bookings always come first, so booking is the only way to guarantee a time. The home page shows the next free chair in real time.

    How do I change or cancel my appointment?

    Call or text us at least 12 hours before your appointment and we’ll move it for free. You can also cancel from the “Your bookings” list on the booking page on the device you booked with. See our cancellation policy.

    How do I add my booking to my calendar?

    After you confirm, you’ll get one-tap buttons for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar (.ics) and Outlook. The event includes your service, barber, the exact start and end time, our address and your booking reference, plus a reminder one hour before.

    What if I’m running late?

    We hold your chair for 10 minutes. After that we may need to shorten your service or rebook you so the next client isn’t kept waiting.

    Do you cut kids’ hair?

    Absolutely. Kids Cut is for ages 12 and under, and Rudo is our go-to for first haircuts. Anyone under 16 needs a parent or guardian in the shop.

    How does the first-visit discount work?

    New clients get 20% off any single service with code FirstFade26 at checkout. It can’t be used on packages or combined with other offers. Full details in our promotion terms.
