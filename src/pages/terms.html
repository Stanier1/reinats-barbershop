1. About these terms

These terms apply to every appointment booked with Reinat’s Barber Studio (Pvt) Ltd (“Reinat’s”, “we”, “us”), 27 Kingsmead Road, Borrowdale, Harare, Zimbabwe — whether you book online, by phone, by text or in person, and to walk-in services.

By confirming a booking you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t book; you’re always welcome to call us with questions first.

2. Making a booking

  • A booking is confirmed when you see a confirmation screen with a booking reference (for example RB-7KQ2M). Please keep this reference.
  • You must give accurate contact details so we can reach you if something changes. One booking covers one person and one service unless you choose a package that says otherwise.
  • Appointment times shown on our website are in Harare time (CAT, UTC+2). Calendar files we provide convert automatically to your device’s time zone.
  • If you book “Any barber”, we assign the first available barber when you confirm. The barber’s name appears on your confirmation.
  • We may occasionally need to change your barber or time because of illness or emergencies. We will contact you as early as possible and offer another time, another barber or a full cancellation.

3. Cancellations & changes

  • Free changes up to 12 hours before. You can reschedule or cancel at no cost by calling or texting +263 77 255 5018 at least 12 hours before your appointment, or by cancelling from “Your upcoming bookings” on our booking page on the device you booked with.
  • Late cancellations (less than 12 hours’ notice) are recorded on your client profile. After two late cancellations in 90 days we may ask you to prepay 50% of future bookings.
  • Groom’s Day and group bookings of three or more people need 48 hours’ notice to change or cancel.
  • If we cancel your appointment for any reason, you will not be charged anything and we will prioritise rebooking you.

4. Late arrivals & no-shows

  • We hold your chair for 10 minutes after your start time. After that we may shorten your service so the next client isn’t delayed, or rebook you for the next free slot. The full service price still applies if the service is shortened because of a late arrival.
  • If you don’t arrive and don’t tell us, the booking is marked as a no-show. After two no-shows we may decline future online bookings or ask for prepayment.
  • If we keep you waiting more than 10 minutes past your booked time, your next Line-Up is free.

5. Prices & payment

  • All prices are in US dollars and include any applicable taxes. The price shown when you book is the price you pay, unless you change or add services on the day.
  • Payment is taken in the studio after your service. We accept cash (USD), Visa, Mastercard and EcoCash. We don’t take payment online.
  • Very long, dense or matted hair may need extra time. Your barber will always confirm any change in price with you before starting.
  • We may update our prices from time to time. Price changes never affect bookings that are already confirmed.

6. Promotions & codes

  • The first-visit code FIRSTFADE gives 20% off one single service for a client who has not visited Reinat’s before. It can be used once per person.
  • It cannot be used on packages (The Full Reinat’s, Cut & Beard, Father & Son, Groom’s Day), combined with other offers, or exchanged for cash.
  • We may ask returning clients who use a first-visit code to pay the full price in the studio.
  • We may end or change promotions at any time, but a discount shown on a confirmed booking will be honoured.

7. Children & minors

  • Kids Cut and Father & Son are for children aged 12 and under.
  • Clients under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian who stays in the studio for the whole appointment.
  • We won’t use a straight razor on clients under 16, and we won’t colour the hair of anyone under 16.

8. Health, allergies & safety

  • Please tell us before your service about any skin condition, allergy, sensitivity, recent scalp treatment or medical condition that could be affected by clippers, razors, heat or products.
  • Colour services need a patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment. It’s free and takes five minutes — just drop in.
  • We may refuse or stop a service if we see an infection, open wound, head lice or a condition that makes it unsafe. There’s no charge if we have to decline before starting.
  • We use single-use blades and disinfect all tools between clients.

9. Satisfaction & free line-ups

  • If you’re not happy with your cut, tell us before you leave or within 3 days and we’ll fix it for free.
  • Every haircut includes a free neck and edge tidy (Line-Up) within 7 days. Just walk in during opening hours; no booking needed, subject to a chair being free.
  • We don’t offer refunds for services that have been completed, but we will always try to put things right.

10. Conduct in the studio

Reinat’s is a relaxed, respectful space for everyone. We may refuse service or ask anyone to leave who is abusive, threatening or under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Please keep children supervised and phone calls short.

11. Photos & social media

  • We love sharing fresh cuts on Instagram and TikTok, but we only film or photograph you with your permission. Your barber will always ask first.
  • If you agree and later change your mind, message us and we’ll remove the post within 7 days.
  • You’re welcome to take your own photos and videos of your cut. Please don’t film other clients without their consent.

12. Liability

We aren’t responsible for reactions to products or treatments when relevant health information wasn’t shared with us, or when a required patch test wasn’t done. Please don’t leave valuables unattended — we can’t accept responsibility for personal belongings.

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act [Chapter 14:44] of Zimbabwe or any liability that can’t legally be limited.

13. Your data

We use your name, phone number and email only to manage your appointments and contact you about them. Read our full Privacy Policy to see how we store and protect your information.

These terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe, and the courts of Harare have jurisdiction. We may update these terms; the version shown on this page when you confirm a booking applies to that booking.
